Semantic Underwater Optical Wireless Communication Using Variational Autoencoders.
Submitted


Mohamed Nennouche1, 2 Mohammad-Ali Khalighi2, 3, Alexis Alfredo Dowhuszko4, Djamal Merad1,5

1Laboratoire d'Informatique et des Systèmes (LIS) 2Centrale Méditerranée 3Fresnel Institute 4Aalto University 5Aix-Marseille University

GitHub Paper


Abstract

The emergence of Internet of underwater things has increased the demand for high-quality underwater image transmission across various applications, including infrastructure inspection and environmental monitoring. Although underwater wireless optical communication (UWOC) offers high data rates, it faces significant challenges due to channel effects that can severely degrade the quality of data transmission. This paper presents a novel deep learning (DL)-based semantic communication approach for underwater image transmission that exploits a variational autoencoder architecture in order to ensure robust performance against channel impairments. This proposed approach employs end-to-end training that jointly optimizes source coding and channel adaptation through neural network optimization, enabling transmission of semantically relevant content while adapting to channel variations. Extensive simulation results across different channel conditions show that our DL-driven semantic technique enables superior image reconstruction over longer transmission ranges, compared to the traditional approach of using image compression with powerful error correction coding. This demonstrates the potential of DL-powered semantic communication as a transformative solution for bandwidth-efficient, optical-based underwater image transmission.
